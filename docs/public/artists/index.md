**SHERE MUSIC Studio** is where musicians manage everything they publish. Open it from your account menu (**SHERE MUSIC STUDIO**, or **Become an artist** if you don't have an artist profile yet).

![SHERE MUSIC Studio](studio.png)

| Studio page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Your latest numbers, recent songs, videos and new followers |
| My Music | Every song you've uploaded, with its status |
| Upload Music | Add a new song |
| Lyrics | Write and sync lyrics for your songs |
| Albums | Group songs into albums |
| Music Videos | Upload videos and subtitles |
| Analytics | Plays, downloads, video views and new followers over time |
| Followers | Who follows your artist profiles |
| Artists | Your artist profiles and verification |
| Payments | Your music submission payments |

Studio only ever shows your own artist profiles and music.

@article creating-an-artist-profile
title: Creating an artist profile
summary: Become an artist on SHERE MUSIC in one step.
keywords: become an artist, artist account, artist profile, musician, band, creator, how do i become an artist

1. Sign in, then choose **Become an artist** in your account menu (or open Studio).
2. Enter your **Artist name**. Add a **Biography**, **Location** and **Artist photo** if you like. You can add a header image and social links later in **Studio → Artists**.
3. Select **Create artist profile**.

Your account becomes an artist account and Studio opens. Your artist profile page is public and listeners can follow it.

## More than one profile

You can manage up to 10 artist profiles — for example a band and a solo project. Add more in **Studio → Artists**.

## Getting verified

A **verified** badge shows listeners that an artist profile is authentic. To ask for it, go to **Studio → Artists** and select **Request verification** on your profile, with a short message. The SHERE MUSIC team reviews each request.

> **Note:** Paying a music submission fee is not verification. Verification is a separate review.

> **Note:** If artist sign-ups are closed, the SHERE MUSIC team can still give you artist access. See [Contact Support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support).

@article uploading-music
title: Uploading music
summary: Add a new song with its audio, artwork and details.
keywords: upload, upload music, add song, audio file, mp3, wav, artwork, cover, release

1. In Studio, choose **Upload Music**.
2. Fill in the **Song information**: title and artist (required), and optionally album, genre, release date, track number and description.
3. Optionally paste the **Lyrics** — plain text, or LRC with timestamps for synced lyrics.
4. Under **Files**, choose the **audio file** and the **artwork**.
5. Decide whether to continue to submission after the upload (see below), then select **Upload song**.

## File requirements

| File | Formats | Maximum size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audio | MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC | {{maxAudioMb}} MB |
| Artwork | JPG, PNG, WebP (square works best) | {{maxImageMb}} MB |

A progress bar shows the upload. You can cancel it while it's uploading.

## Drafts

A new song is saved as a **private draft**. Nobody else can see it until it's submitted, reviewed and published. You can edit a draft, replace its audio or artwork, or delete it at any time from **My Music**.

## Next step

[Submit your music for review](/help/artists/submitting-music-for-review).

@article adding-lyrics
title: Adding lyrics
summary: Write, import and sync lyrics for your songs.
keywords: add lyrics, lyrics editor, sync lyrics, lrc, timestamps, how to add lyrics

Open **Studio → Lyrics** and choose a song (or select **Lyrics** on a song's edit page).

## Write or import

- **Lines** — type or paste the lyrics, one line per lyric line.
- **Import / export** — paste plain text or an LRC file (lines like `[00:12.50]First line`), or **Export .lrc** to download your synced lyrics.

## Sync the lyrics to the music

The **Sync** tab lets you add a start time to every line while the song plays:

1. Press [[Space]] to play or pause.
2. Press [[Enter]] the moment each line starts. The next line is selected automatically.
3. Check the result in the **Preview** tab.

You can change these keys in the Sync tab.

## Details

Add a **copyright notice** and **attribution** if needed. They're shown under the lyrics.

## Save and submit

- **Save** keeps your lyrics as a draft.
- **Save & submit for review** sends them to the SHERE MUSIC team. They appear to listeners once approved.

You can add lyrics in more than one language — choose **New language** in the editor.

@article uploading-music-videos
title: Uploading music videos
summary: Upload a video, thumbnail and subtitles.
keywords: music video, upload video, mp4, thumbnail, subtitles, captions, srt, vtt

1. In Studio, open **Music Videos** and select **New video**.
2. Add the title and artist, and optionally a linked song, genre, release date, description and **Thumbnail** image. Save the details.
3. Select **Upload video** and choose the file (MP4, WebM or MOV, up to {{maxVideoMb}} MB). Keep the page open until the upload finishes.
4. Add **subtitles** in as many languages as you like — upload a `.srt` or `.vtt` file for each language.
5. Submit the video for review.

Like songs, a video goes live after the SHERE MUSIC team approves it (you then publish it, or the team publishes it for you).

@article submitting-music-for-review
title: Submitting music for review
summary: How your songs go from draft to live.
keywords: submit, review, pending, approved, published, rejected, changes requested, status, go live

Every new song is checked by the SHERE MUSIC team before listeners can hear it.

## Submit a song

From the song's edit page (or **My Music**), select **Submit for review — {{submissionFee}}**. You'll see a summary of the song and the fee, then pay securely. See [Artist payment](/help/artists/artist-payment).

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Private. Only you can see it. |
| Pending review | Submitted and waiting for the SHERE MUSIC team. |
| Approved | Accepted. Publish it whenever you're ready. |
| Published | Live on SHERE MUSIC. |
| Changes requested | Not approved. The reason is shown on the song — fix it and submit again. |

We notify you (in SHERE MUSIC and by email, depending on your [notification settings](/help/settings/notifications)) when your submission is reviewed.

## Editing a published song

Editing an approved or published song sends it back for review. While it's being reviewed it's hidden from listeners, and it goes live again once the changes are approved.

> **Note:** Paying doesn't guarantee approval. Songs must follow the [Community Guidelines](/help/legal/community-guidelines) and the [Copyright Policy](/help/legal/copyright-policy).

@article artist-payment
title: Artist payment
summary: The music submission fee, receipts and what happens if a payment fails.
keywords: submission fee, artist payment, pay to submit, receipt, payment failed, retry, 500

Submitting a song for review has a one-time **music submission fee of {{submissionFee}}** per submission.

## Pay and submit

1. Select **Submit for review** on your song.
2. On **Submit your music**, check the song, artist and fee.
3. Select **Pay {{submissionFee}} & Submit**. Paystack's secure checkout opens.
4. After a successful payment you'll see **Payment successful**, your payment reference and the status **Submitted for review**. We also email you a receipt.

## If the payment fails

You'll see **Payment was not completed**. Your song has **not** been submitted and your draft is safe. Select **Try again** — you won't create a duplicate submission.

## Receipts

**Studio → Payments** lists each submission with its amount, payment status, review status, payment reference and date.

## Good to know

- The fee covers one review. If your song is sent back with **Changes requested**, a new submission needs a new fee.
- Editing a song that is already approved or published doesn't need a new fee.
- The fee isn't artist verification and doesn't guarantee approval.
- SHERE MUSIC never sees or stores your card details.

@article artist-analytics
title: Artist analytics
summary: See how your music is doing.
keywords: analytics, stats, statistics, plays, downloads, video views, followers, insights, top songs

**Studio → Overview** shows your totals at a glance: songs (live and in review), plays, downloads, followers, albums and video views, plus a 14-day activity chart.

**Studio → Analytics** goes further. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months** to see:

- plays, downloads, video views and new followers for the period
- a day-by-day chart
- your top songs and top videos

**Studio → Followers** lists the people following your artist profiles (people with private profiles appear anonymously).

Analytics only ever include your own artist profiles and music.
