SHERE MUSIC is a place to discover, stream and download music, read song lyrics and watch music videos.

![The SHERE MUSIC home page](home.png)

## What you can do for free

- **Listen** to every published song, as often as you like.
- **Read lyrics**, including lyrics that scroll along with the song.
- **Make playlists** and save songs to your **Favorites**.
- **Follow artists** and see their new releases in your Following feed.
- **Watch music videos**, with subtitles when the artist provides them.

You can browse and listen without an account. Create a free account to save favorites, build playlists, follow artists and keep your settings on every device.

## SHERE MUSIC Plus

[SHERE MUSIC Plus](/help/plus/what-is-plus) is a monthly membership ({{plusPrice}} per month) that lets you download music to your device.

## For musicians

Artists upload their own music, lyrics and music videos in [SHERE MUSIC Studio](/help/artists/shere-music-studio). Every new song is checked by the SHERE MUSIC team before it goes live.

## Sign up with your email

1. Select **Sign up** at the top of any page.
2. Enter your name, email address and a password. Your password needs at least 8 characters, including a letter and a number.
3. Select **Create account**.
4. Open the email we send you and select **Verify email address**. The link works for 24 hours.

You can sign in as soon as your email address is verified.

> **Tip:** Didn't get the email? Check your spam or promotions folder. On the sign-in page you can ask for a new verification email.

## Sign up with Google or Facebook

Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Facebook** on the sign-up page. See [Google login](/help/getting-started/google-login) and [Facebook login](/help/getting-started/facebook-login).

## Sign in

1. Select **Log in** at the top of the page.
2. Enter your email address and password, then select **Log in**.

You stay signed in on that browser for several days. To sign out, open the menu under your profile picture and select **Sign out**.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address. If it belongs to an account, we send you a reset link.
3. Open the link (it works for 1 hour) and choose a new password.

Resetting your password signs you out on every other device, to keep your account safe.

## "Please verify your email address"

Your account isn't active until you confirm your email. Select **Resend verification email** on the sign-in page, then open the newest email.

1. On the sign-in or sign-up page, select **Continue with Google**.
2. Choose your Google account and allow SHERE MUSIC to see your name and email address.
3. You're sent back to SHERE MUSIC and signed in.

We only use Google to confirm who you are. We never see your Google password.

## Already have a SHERE MUSIC account?

If your Google email address matches your existing account, Google is added to that account. You can also connect Google yourself in **Settings → Connected Accounts** while signed in.

> **Note:** Google and Facebook sign-in only appear when they are available on SHERE MUSIC. If you don't see the buttons, sign in with your email and password.

@article facebook-login
title: Facebook login
summary: Use your Facebook account to sign in to SHERE MUSIC.
keywords: facebook, social login, sign in with facebook

1. On the sign-in or sign-up page, select **Continue with Facebook**.
2. Confirm on Facebook that SHERE MUSIC may see your name and email address.
3. You're sent back to SHERE MUSIC and signed in.

If your Facebook account has no confirmed email address, or it matches an account that isn't verified yet, SHERE MUSIC asks you to sign in with your password first. You can then connect Facebook in **Settings → Connected Accounts**.

## Stop using Facebook to sign in

Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts** and select **Disconnect** next to Facebook. If Facebook is your only way to sign in, add a password first in **Settings → Security**.

## Edit your profile

Go to **Settings → Profile** to change:

- your profile picture
- your display name and username
- your biography, location and website
- your social links and favourite genres (used for "Made for you" on the home page)

## Your public profile page

When you choose a username, your profile gets its own page at `/u/your-username`. People can see your name, picture, bio and public playlists.

You decide what else appears there, such as your recently played songs or the artists you follow, in **Settings → Privacy**. Turn off **Public profile** to hide the page completely.

## Your Library

Your **Library** (from the menu) shows your account summary, recently played songs and download history. It is only visible to you.
