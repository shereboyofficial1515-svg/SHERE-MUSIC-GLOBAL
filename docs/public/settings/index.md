Open **Settings** from your account menu. On a phone, Settings shows a list of categories; tap one to open it.

![Settings](settings.png)

## Account

**Settings → Account** shows your email, account type, status, when you joined and how you sign in.

### Change your email

1. In **Change email**, enter the new address and your current password.
2. Open the confirmation link we send to the **new** address.

Your email only changes after you confirm it.

### Delete your account

**Delete account** permanently removes your account, favorites, playlists and history. Enter your password to confirm. If you own artist profiles, remove or hand them over first. This can't be undone.

## Language

**Settings → Language** sets your preferred lyrics language and subtitle language.

## Settings when signed out

Playback, appearance, accessibility, download and language settings also work when you're not signed in; they're saved on that device only. Sign in to keep them on every device.

**Settings → Profile** lets you change your profile picture, display name, username (3–30 letters, numbers, dots or underscores), biography, location, website, social links and favourite genres.

Your favourite genres help fill **Made for you** on your home page. See also [Your profile](/help/getting-started/your-profile).

**Settings → Music & Playback**:

- **Autoplay** — when your queue ends, keep playing similar music.
- **Remember playback position** — pick up where you left off.
- **Open lyrics automatically** — the expanded player opens on the Lyrics tab for songs that have lyrics.

title: Appearance

**Settings → Appearance** → **Theme**:

- **Light** — bright surfaces, dark text
- **Dark** — easy on the eyes at night
- **System** — follows your phone or computer's setting

You can also switch quickly with the theme button at the top of the page.

@article accessibility
title: Accessibility

**Settings → Accessibility**:

- **Reduce motion** — turns off page transitions, lyric animations and the player visualiser. By default it follows your device.
- **Larger text** — increases the size of all text.
- **High contrast** — stronger text and borders, no see-through surfaces.
- **Larger controls** — bigger buttons and touch targets.
- **Show captions by default** and **Preferred caption language** — for music videos.
- **Persistent lyrics** — keep the lyrics tab open in the player for every song.

SHERE MUSIC also works with keyboards and screen readers: every control has a label, and the video player has [keyboard shortcuts](/help/music-video/video-player).

@article notifications
title: Notifications
summary: Choose which notifications and emails you get.
keywords: notifications, alerts, emails, bell, new music, turn off emails

The bell at the top of the page shows your notifications. In **Settings → Notifications** choose what you get:

- **New music** — an artist you follow releases a song
- **New music videos** — an artist you follow releases a video
- **Artist updates** — news from artists you follow
- **Followers** — someone follows your artist profile
- **Account & reviews** — review decisions on your uploads, verification and account changes

**Email notifications** also sends these to your inbox. Security emails (such as password changes) and payment receipts are always sent.

@article privacy
title: Privacy
summary: Control your public profile and how your listening is used.
keywords: privacy, public profile, private, listening activity, recommendations, analytics, data

**Settings → Privacy**:

- **Public profile** — when off, your profile page is hidden and you appear anonymously in artists' follower lists.
- **Show listening activity** — show your recently played songs on your public profile.
- **Show who you follow** — list the artists you follow on your profile.
- **Show your followers** (artists) — let visitors see who follows your artist profiles. The count is always shown.
- **Personalised recommendations** — use your listening history and favorites for "Made for you" and "Because you listened".
- **Usage analytics** — when off, your plays are counted anonymously and no listening history is kept.

Read more in the [Privacy Policy](/help/legal/privacy-policy).

@article downloads

**Settings → Downloads**:

- **Download notifications** — show a notice when a download starts and finishes.
- **Only download on Wi-Fi** — block downloads on mobile data. This only works in browsers that report the connection type (mostly Chrome on Android); elsewhere the option is greyed out.
- **Download history** — songs you've downloaded while signed in.

Downloading music needs [SHERE MUSIC Plus](/help/plus/downloading-music). Where files are saved is decided by your browser or phone.

## Change your password

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Enter your current password, then your new password twice (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number).
3. Select **Update password**.

Changing your password signs you out on every other device.

If you only sign in with Google or Facebook, you'll see **Set a password** instead, so you can also sign in with your email.

## Sign out everywhere else

Under **Sessions**, select **Sign out of all other devices**. (The same option is under **Settings → Account → Sign out**.) You stay signed in on the one you're using.

## Connected accounts

**Settings → Connected Accounts** shows whether Google or Facebook can sign you in, and lets you connect or disconnect them.

> **Tip:** Never share your password. SHERE MUSIC staff will never ask for it.
