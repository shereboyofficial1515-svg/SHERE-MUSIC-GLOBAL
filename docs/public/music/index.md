## Home

The home page shows the Spotlight, trending songs, new releases, popular artists, genres, featured playlists and music videos. When you're signed in you also get **Recently played**, **Made for you**, **Because you listened** and new music from artists you follow.

## Discover

**Discover** lists the whole catalog. Pick a genre, then sort by **Recently added**, **New releases**, **Most played**, **Most downloaded** or **Title A–Z**. More songs load as you scroll.

## Search

Select the search icon (or **Search** in the menu) and start typing. Results are grouped into songs, artists, albums, playlists, music videos, genres and **lyrics** — so you can find a song from a line you remember.

## Artists, albums and genres

- An **artist page** shows popular songs, latest releases, albums and music videos, and lets you follow the artist.
- An **album page** lists its songs in order.
- A **genre page** shows every song in that genre.

@article playing-music
title: Playing music
summary: Start a song, a whole list, or a song from a specific line.
keywords: play, listen, stream, start, song, shuffle play

- Select **Play** on a song, or the play button that appears on a song's artwork.
- Selecting play on a song in a list (an album, playlist or search result) plays that list from that song.
- On a song page, select **Play** to start it. If the song has synced lyrics, select any lyric line to start playing from there.

Music keeps playing while you move around SHERE MUSIC.

> **Tip:** Songs stream over the internet, so you need a connection to listen. [SHERE MUSIC Plus](/help/plus/downloading-music) members can download songs to keep a copy on their device.

## The player bar

While a song is playing, the player bar stays at the bottom of the screen with play/pause, previous, next, a progress bar you can drag to jump, and volume (on larger screens).

## Shuffle and repeat

- **Shuffle** plays the rest of your queue in random order.
- **Repeat** cycles through **Repeat off**, **Repeat all** (start the queue again when it ends) and **Repeat one** (play the current song again).

## The expanded player

Select the song title or artwork in the player bar to open the full-screen player. It matches its colours to the song's artwork and has three tabs:

- **Lyrics** — lyrics that follow the music, when available
- **Up next** — your queue
- **Info** — details about the song

Press [[Esc]] or select the close button to go back.

## Lock screen and headphones

On phones and most computers, the song shows on your lock screen and media keys, so you can pause or skip without opening SHERE MUSIC.

## Autoplay and resume

When your queue ends, **Autoplay** keeps playing similar music. SHERE MUSIC can also remember where you stopped. Change both in [Settings → Music & Playback](/help/settings/playback).

## Add songs to the queue

Open the **⋯** menu on any song and choose:

- **Play next** — plays straight after the current song
- **Add to queue** — adds it to the end

## See and change the queue

Open the expanded player and choose **Up next**. From there you can:

- select a song to play it now
- move songs up or down
- remove songs from the queue

You need a free account to make playlists.

## Create a playlist

1. Go to **Playlists** and select **New playlist**.
2. Give it a name and (optionally) a description.
3. Choose whether it's a **Public playlist** — anyone with the link can view and play it — or private.

## Add songs

Open the **⋯** menu on a song, choose **Add to playlist**, then pick a playlist (or create a new one right there).

## Edit a playlist

On your playlist's page you can rename it, change the description, switch between public and private, upload a cover image, remove songs, or delete the playlist.

## Featured playlists

The SHERE MUSIC team features some public playlists on the home page.

@article favorites
title: Favorites
summary: Save songs you love with the heart button.
keywords: favorite, favourites, like, heart, saved songs, liked songs

Select the **heart** on any song (or **Add to favorites** in its **⋯** menu). Your favorites are listed on the **Favorites** page, newest first. Select the heart again to remove a song.

Favorites need a free account, and they're saved to your account, so they appear on every device you sign in on.

@article recently-played
title: Recently played
summary: Find songs you listened to recently.
keywords: history, recently played, listening history, recent

When you're signed in, SHERE MUSIC remembers what you play:

- **Home** shows a **Recently played** row.
- **Library** (in the menu, also called **History**) has a **Recently played** tab.

Your listening history is private. You can show your recently played songs on your public profile with **Show listening activity** in [Settings → Privacy](/help/settings/privacy).

> **Note:** If you turn off **Usage analytics** in Settings → Privacy, your plays are counted anonymously and no listening history is kept.
