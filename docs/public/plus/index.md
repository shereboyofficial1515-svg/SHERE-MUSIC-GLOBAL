## Download music to your device

Save the original audio file of published songs to your phone or computer. See [Downloading music](/help/plus/downloading-music).

## Plus-only offers

The **SHERE MUSIC Plus** page lists current offers. Offers marked PLUS show their full details and link only to members; others see them locked.

## PLUS badge

A small **PLUS** badge appears next to your name in your account menu, Library and Billing page.

> **Note:** Offline listening *inside* the SHERE MUSIC website isn't available yet. Songs you download play in your device's own music player.

@article how-to-subscribe
title: How to subscribe
summary: Join Plus in a few steps, paying securely through Paystack.
keywords: subscribe, join plus, buy plus, upgrade, pay, get plus, checkout, card

1. Sign in (or create a free account).
2. Go to **SHERE MUSIC Plus** in the menu, or select **Download** on any song and choose **Get Plus**.
3. Select **Get SHERE MUSIC PLUS**. You're taken to Paystack's secure checkout.
4. Pay with your card or another option Paystack offers.
5. You're brought back to SHERE MUSIC. When your payment is confirmed you'll see **Welcome to SHERE MUSIC Plus**, and we email you a receipt.

Your membership starts straight away and renews every month on the same date.

> **Important:** SHERE MUSIC never sees or stores your card details — Paystack handles them.

## "We're still confirming your payment"

Sometimes confirmation takes a moment. If you completed the payment, it's confirmed automatically — **don't pay again**. Select **Check again** after a minute, or look in [Settings → Billing & Membership](/settings/billing).

@article downloading-music
title: Downloading music
summary: How Plus members save songs to their device.
keywords: download, download music, save song, offline, mp3, device, how do i download

1. Make sure you're signed in with your Plus membership.
2. Select **Download** on a song page, the download button on a song, or **Download** in a song's **⋯** menu.
3. A progress indicator shows while the file downloads. When it's finished you'll see **Download complete**.

Your browser saves the file to its usual downloads folder (on phones, often the **Downloads** or **Files** app). It's the original audio file, named "Artist - Title".

## Without Plus

Download buttons are marked **PLUS**. Selecting one opens a short explanation and a **Get Plus** button — nothing is downloaded.

## Download settings

In [Settings → Downloads](/help/settings/downloads) you can turn download notifications on or off, and choose to download only on Wi-Fi (on browsers that can tell).

## Download history

See what you've downloaded in **Library → Download history** or **Settings → Downloads**.

> **Note:** Downloads are for your personal listening. Please respect the artists' rights — don't share or re-upload downloaded music.

@article managing-your-subscription
title: Managing your subscription
summary: See your plan, next billing date and receipts, or update your card.
keywords: billing, membership, next billing date, receipt, payment history, update card, change card, manage subscription

Go to **Settings → Billing & Membership** (or select **Billing & Membership** in your account menu).

- **Current plan** shows Plus, the price, the status and your **next billing date**.
- **Manage subscription** opens Paystack's secure page where you can update the card used for renewals.
- **Payment history** lists every payment with its reference, amount, status and date. Card details are never shown.

## If a renewal fails

If Paystack can't charge your card, your plan shows **Payment failed** and we email you. Update your card with **Manage subscription**. Your Plus benefits stop when your paid month ends unless the renewal succeeds.

@article cancelling-plus
title: Cancelling Plus
summary: Stop your membership from renewing.
keywords: cancel, cancel plus, stop subscription, end membership, unsubscribe, refund

1. Go to **Settings → Billing & Membership**.
2. Select **Cancel subscription**, then confirm.

Your membership won't renew. **You keep Plus until the end of the month you've already paid for**, then your account goes back to the Free plan. We send you an email to confirm.

Your playlists, favorites and everything else stay exactly as they are. You can join Plus again at any time.

> **Note:** Songs you already downloaded stay on your device.
