@article music-wont-play
title: Music won't play
summary: What to try when a song doesn't start or keeps stopping.
keywords: not playing, won't play, no sound, buffering, stops, error, audio problem

1. **Check your connection.** Songs stream over the internet. Try another website or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
2. **Check the volume.** Make sure SHERE MUSIC's player isn't muted and your device volume is up. On phones, check the silent switch.
3. **Reload the page.** Playback links expire after a few hours; reloading gets a fresh one.
4. **Try another song.** If only one song fails, it may have been removed or be temporarily unavailable.
5. **Update your browser.** SHERE MUSIC works best in recent versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

> **Tip:** If music stops when your phone's screen turns off, check that battery-saving settings aren't closing your browser in the background.

## It asks me to get Plus

Downloading to your device is a [SHERE MUSIC Plus](/help/plus/what-is-plus) benefit. If you are a member:

- make sure you're signed in to the account that has Plus
- check **Settings → Billing & Membership** — if your membership ended or your renewal failed, Plus is no longer active

## It says I'm on mobile data

You turned on **Only download on Wi-Fi**. Connect to Wi-Fi, or turn it off in **Settings → Downloads**.

## The download stopped

Downloads need a steady connection until they finish. Select **Download** again.

## I can't find the file

Your browser saves downloads in its downloads folder. On Android, open the **Files** or **Downloads** app; on iPhone, the **Files** app → **Downloads**; on a computer, your **Downloads** folder.

@article login-problems
title: Login problems
summary: Can't sign in? Start here.
keywords: can't log in, cannot sign in, login problem, wrong password, verify email, account disabled, session expired

- **"Incorrect email or password"** — check for typos and Caps Lock, or [reset your password](/help/getting-started/logging-in).
- **"Please verify your email address"** — select **Resend verification email** and open the newest email (check spam).
- **You normally use Google or Facebook** — use the same button again. If you want to use your email too, set a password in Settings → Security after signing in.
- **"Your session has expired"** — sign in again. This happens after a few days, or after your password was changed.
- **"This account has been disabled"** — [contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support).

## My payment was declined

Paystack couldn't complete the payment. Check your card details and balance, or try another card or payment method. Nothing is charged for a declined payment.

## I paid but don't have Plus (or my song wasn't submitted)

1. Open the page you were sent back to, or go to **Settings → Billing & Membership** (Plus) or **Studio → Payments** (artists).
2. If the payment shows as **Pending**, wait a minute — confirmation is automatic. **Don't pay again.**
3. If it's still not right after a few minutes, [contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support) with your **payment reference** (it starts with `SM-`). You'll find it on the payment page, in your receipt email, and in your payment history.

## My Plus renewal failed

Update your card with **Manage subscription** in Settings → Billing & Membership.

> **Important:** SHERE MUSIC staff will never ask for your card number, PIN or one-time passcode.

@article lyrics-problems
title: Lyrics problems
summary: Missing lyrics, lyrics out of time, or the wrong language.
keywords: lyrics missing, lyrics out of sync, wrong lyrics, lyrics not showing, lyrics timing

- **No lyrics** — not every song has lyrics yet. See [Lyrics availability](/help/lyrics/lyrics-availability).
- **Lyrics don't move with the song** — the song only has plain lyrics without timing.
- **Timing is off** — let the artist or support know. If you're the artist, re-sync the lines in Studio → Lyrics.
- **Wrong language** — change **Preferred lyrics language** in Settings → Language.
- **Mistakes in the lyrics** — artists can correct their own lyrics in Studio; otherwise, [contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support).

@article video-problems
title: Video problems
summary: Videos that won't play, missing subtitles, or no picture-in-picture.
keywords: video not playing, video buffering, subtitles missing, captions, picture in picture missing, fullscreen not working

- **The video won't start or keeps buffering** — check your connection and reload the page. Large videos need a steady connection.
- **No subtitles** — the artist hasn't added subtitles for this video, or not in your language.
- **No picture-in-picture button** — your browser doesn't support it.
- **Full screen doesn't work on iPhone** — make sure you're using the latest iOS; try rotating your phone.
- **The Videos section is missing** — music videos may be turned off on SHERE MUSIC for a while.
