@article viewing-lyrics
title: Viewing lyrics
summary: Where to find the lyrics for a song.
keywords: lyrics, words, song text, read lyrics, how lyrics work

You can read lyrics in two places:

- **On the song page** — scroll down to the **Lyrics** card.
- **In the expanded player** — open the player and choose the **Lyrics** tab.

![Lyrics on a song page](lyrics.png)

Some lyrics show who wrote or provided them, and a copyright notice, underneath.

> **Tip:** Want the Lyrics tab to open every time you open the player? Turn on **Open lyrics automatically** in [Settings → Music & Playback](/help/settings/playback).

@article synchronized-lyrics
title: Synchronized lyrics
summary: Lyrics that highlight each line as it's sung.
keywords: synced lyrics, synchronized, karaoke, time, highlight, follow along, jump to line

Many songs have **synced lyrics**: the line being sung is highlighted and the lyrics scroll by themselves while the song plays.

- **Jump to a line:** select any line to play the song from that point.
- **Scroll freely:** you can scroll through the lyrics while the song plays; they catch up with the music again shortly after.

Songs without timing information show the lyrics as plain text instead.

@article full-screen-lyrics
title: Full-screen lyrics
summary: Lyrics mode shows big, easy-to-read lyrics over the whole screen.
keywords: lyrics mode, full screen lyrics, big lyrics, karaoke mode

1. Open a song page (or the expanded player) for a song that has lyrics.
2. Select **Lyrics mode**.
3. The lyrics fill the screen with large text, in colours taken from the song's artwork, with play, pause, skip, shuffle and repeat controls at the bottom.

To leave, select **Exit lyrics mode** or press [[Esc]].

> **Note:** If you turned on **Reduce motion** in Settings → Accessibility, lyrics move without animation.

@article lyrics-availability
title: Lyrics availability
summary: Why some songs have lyrics and others don't.
keywords: no lyrics, missing lyrics, lyrics not showing, language, translation

Lyrics are added by the artists who upload their music, or by the SHERE MUSIC team, and every set of lyrics is checked before it appears. So:

- a new song may not have lyrics yet
- some songs have plain lyrics but no timing
- some songs have lyrics in more than one language

## Choose a lyrics language

When a song has lyrics in several languages, SHERE MUSIC shows your preferred language first. Set it in [Settings → Language](/help/settings/account).

## Lyrics from a lyrics provider

For some songs, SHERE MUSIC can show lyrics supplied by a licensed lyrics service. These are marked with the provider's name.

## Are you the artist?

Add or fix your lyrics in SHERE MUSIC Studio. See [Adding lyrics](/help/artists/adding-lyrics).
