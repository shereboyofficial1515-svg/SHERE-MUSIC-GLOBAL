## What we collect

| Information | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Name, email address, password (stored only as a secure one-way hash) | To create and secure your account |
| Profile details you choose to add (picture, username, bio, location, website, social links, favourite genres) | To show your profile and personalise recommendations |
| Favorites, playlists, follows and settings | To provide those features |
| Songs you play and download, while signed in | Your history, recommendations, and play and download counts |
| Search words (without who searched) | To improve search and see what's popular |
| Payment records: reference, amount, currency, status and date | Receipts, support and accounting |
| For artists: uploaded music, artwork, lyrics, videos and artist profile details | To publish your work |

We **don't** store your card details — payments are processed by Paystack. Our listening statistics don't store your IP address or device details.

## Your choices

In **Settings → Privacy** you can hide your public profile, choose what it shows, turn off personalised recommendations, and turn off usage analytics (your plays are then counted anonymously and no listening history is kept). In **Settings → Notifications** you choose which emails you get.

## Who we share information with

We don't sell your personal information. We share only what's needed with the service providers that run SHERE MUSIC:

- **Paystack** — to process payments and subscriptions
- our **email delivery** provider — to send account, payment and notification emails
- our **cloud hosting** providers — to store the service's data and files
- **Google** or **Facebook** — only if you choose to sign in with them

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## Public information

Your public profile, public playlists, and (if you're an artist) your artist profile and published music are visible to everyone. Artists see follower counts; they only see followers' names if those followers have public profiles.

## Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account, favorites, playlists and history. Payment records are kept, without your name, for accounting and legal reasons.

SHERE MUSIC respects the rights of artists and songwriters, and expects every uploader to do the same.

## For artists

Only upload music, artwork, lyrics and videos that you created or have permission to publish. Samples, covers and remixes need the permission of the original rights holders. Lyrics must credit the writers and include any copyright notice required.

## Reporting infringement

If you believe content on SHERE MUSIC uses your work without permission, email **{{contactEmail}}** with:

1. your name and contact details
2. the work you own
3. the link to each song, video, lyrics or profile on SHERE MUSIC
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised, and that your report is accurate and you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them
5. your signature (typing your full name is fine)

We review every complete report and may remove or unpublish the content while we look into it. We may tell the uploader and share your report with them.

## Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, reply to our email explaining why and showing that you have the rights.

## Repeat infringement

Accounts that repeatedly upload infringing content may be disabled.

SHERE MUSIC is for music lovers and the artists who make it. Help keep it welcoming.

## Everyone

- Be respectful in your profile, playlist names and descriptions.
- No impersonation — don't pretend to be an artist or person you're not.
- No spam, scams or misleading links.
- Don't try to break, overload or get around the security of SHERE MUSIC.

## Artists and uploads

Content may be rejected or removed if it:

- infringes someone's copyright (see the [Copyright Policy](/help/legal/copyright-policy))
- promotes violence, hatred or discrimination against people or groups
- contains sexual content involving minors, or illegal content of any kind
- harasses or threatens anyone
- is misleading — for example wrong artist names, fake credits, or artwork that isn't yours
- is low quality in a way that hurts listeners, such as silence, noise or duplicate uploads

The SHERE MUSIC team reviews every submission and may request changes, reject it, or remove published content. Accounts that break these guidelines may be disabled.

To report something, contact **{{contactEmail}}** with a link to it.
