@article watching-music-videos
title: Watching music videos
summary: Find and watch music videos on SHERE MUSIC.
keywords: video, music video, watch, videos, browse videos, how to watch

Select **Videos** in the menu to open **SHERE MUSIC VIDEO**.

![SHERE MUSIC VIDEO](video.png)

- The video home shows a featured video, **Trending videos**, the latest videos, new releases, categories and popular artists. When you're signed in you also get recommendations.
- **See all** opens every music video. Filter by genre and sort by **Latest**, **Most viewed**, **Release date** or **Title A–Z**.
- Select a video to watch it. Below the player you'll find the artist (with a **Follow** button), the linked song (**Listen to the song**) and related videos.

Music videos are free to watch for everyone.

@article video-player
title: Video player
summary: Controls and keyboard shortcuts.
keywords: video player, controls, speed, quality, shortcuts, keyboard, volume

The player has play/pause, a progress bar, volume, captions, playback speed, full screen and picture-in-picture. Controls hide while the video plays; move the mouse or tap to show them.

## Playback speed

Choose from 0.5× to 2× in the speed menu.

## Quality

When a video is available in more than one quality, choose it in the **Quality** menu. Otherwise the original quality plays.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| [[Space]] or [[K]] | Play / pause |
| [[←]] / [[→]] | Back / forward 5 seconds |
| [[J]] / [[L]] | Back / forward 10 seconds |
| [[↑]] / [[↓]] | Volume up / down |
| [[M]] | Mute |
| [[C]] | Subtitles on / off |
| [[F]] | Full screen |
| [[Shift]] + [[>]] / [[<]] | Faster / slower |

@article subtitles
title: Subtitles
summary: Turn on captions and choose a language.
keywords: subtitles, captions, cc, language, closed captions

When a video has subtitles, select the **captions** button in the player and choose a language (or press [[C]]).

## Always show subtitles

In [Settings → Accessibility](/help/settings/accessibility), turn on **Show captions by default** and choose your **Preferred caption language**. Videos then start with subtitles in that language when it's available.

Subtitles are added by the artists. Not every video has them, and each video may have different languages.

@article fullscreen
title: Fullscreen
summary: Watch a video using the whole screen.
keywords: full screen, fullscreen, maximise, landscape

Select the **full screen** button in the player, press [[F]], or double-click the video (on computers). Press [[Esc]] or the button again to leave full screen.

On phones, turning your phone sideways while in full screen gives the largest picture.

@article picture-in-picture
title: Picture-in-picture
summary: Keep a video playing in a small window.
keywords: picture in picture, pip, mini player, floating video

Select the **picture-in-picture** button in the player. The video moves into a small floating window that stays on top while you use other tabs or apps. Select it again (or close the small window) to bring the video back.

> **Note:** Picture-in-picture depends on your browser. If you don't see the button, your browser doesn't support it.
