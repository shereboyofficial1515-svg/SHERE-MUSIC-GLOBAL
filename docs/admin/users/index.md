@article user-management
title: User management
summary: Find and open user accounts.
keywords: users, find user, search users, user list

**Users** lists every account. Search by name or email and filter by role (Listeners, Artists, Administrators) and status (Active, Disabled). Select a user to open their details.

@article user-accounts
title: User accounts
summary: What the user detail page shows.
keywords: user details, account details, favorites, downloads, last sign in

A user's page shows their role and status badges, email verification, when they joined, their last sign-in, totals (favorites, playlists, downloads, plays) and their most recent downloads.

Admins can't see or change a user's password. To help someone who's locked out, point them to **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

@article plus-users
title: Plus users
summary: See who has SHERE MUSIC Plus.
keywords: plus users, plus members, subscribers, membership

**Plus Members** (Monetization) lists subscriptions: user, plan, status, start date, current billing period and last payment. **With Plus now** shows only members whose paid period hasn't ended; **All subscriptions (history)** includes past ones. **Details** shows the Paystack subscription and customer codes and every payment.

Memberships only change through Paystack (checkout, renewal, failure, cancellation). There's intentionally no "mark as paid" or "give Plus" button. See [Subscriptions](/admin/docs/payments/subscriptions).

@article account-status
title: Account status
summary: Disable and re-enable accounts.
keywords: disable account, enable account, ban, suspend, status

- **Disable account** — the user is signed out everywhere immediately, can't sign in, and gets an email. Their content stays as it is.
- **Enable account** — lets them sign in again (they get an email).

You can't disable your own account, and the last active administrator can't be disabled.

@article account-moderation
title: Account moderation
summary: When and how to act on an account.
keywords: moderation, abuse, spam account, impersonation, deletion request

- **Spam or abuse** — disable the account; unpublish or delete offending playlists in **Playlists** and content in Music / Music Videos.
- **Impersonation** — remove verification and unpublish, then contact the real artist.
- **Deletion requests** — users can delete their own account in Settings → Account. If they can't, confirm their identity by email before doing anything on their behalf.
- **Artist role** — remove it (Make listener) only after their artist profiles are reassigned or deleted.

Playlist moderation: **Playlists** lets you feature public playlists on the home page, make a playlist private, or delete it.
